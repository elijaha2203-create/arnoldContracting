# Product

## Register

brand

## Platform

web

## Users

Homeowners researching a general contractor for remodeling and finish carpentry work: kitchens, additions, custom built-ins, and whole-home renovations. They're comparing a handful of local contractors, often mid-project-consideration (not yet ready to sign), and they're screening for craftsmanship and trustworthiness before they'll pick up the phone. They browse on both mobile and desktop, frequently while getting quotes from multiple contractors side by side.

## Product Purpose

Arnold Contracting's site is the credibility check a homeowner runs before calling. It exists to turn a cold visitor into a phone call or quote request by proving the quality of the work (through a real project gallery), the character of the business (family-owned, detail-oriented craftsmanship), and the ease of taking the next step. Success is a visitor picking up the phone, emailing, or submitting the quote request — not just browsing.

## Positioning

Every remodel is treated with the same obsessive attention to detail as a family would give their own home — because it is a family-owned business, not a subcontractor pipeline.

## Conversion & proof

- Primary CTA: "Contact Now To Get a Free Quote." Secondary fallback: direct phone number and email, both featured prominently (header and footer at minimum).
- The line a visitor remembers after 10 seconds: Arnold Contracting is a family-owned, detail-obsessed craftsman — not a generic remodeling outfit.
- Belief ladder: (1) this business does real, high-quality craftsmanship work — the gallery proves it; (2) they're an established, licensed, trustworthy operation — certifications and years-in-business prove it; (3) other homeowners vouch for them — testimonials prove it; (4) reaching out is easy and low-risk — the quote CTA and direct contact info remove friction.
- Proof on hand: testimonials, past project photos, certifications/licenses, and years-in-business information are available and should be collected into `.impeccable/assets/proof/` (or referenced by path) as they're supplied.

## Brand Personality

Craftsmanship, detail-oriented, family-owned. Confident and grounded rather than corporate or salesy — the tone of a builder who takes pride in the work, not a franchise pitching volume. Bold, high-contrast typography (per the Kontix reference) carries authority; galleries of real project photography carry proof. Navigation and page structure stay simple and uncluttered — the brief is explicit that the site should never feel like it's overwhelming the visitor with information.

## Anti-references

Do not resemble a generic cheap-looking contractor template. No corporate SaaS aesthetic, no neon colors, no gradients (including gradient text), no overly flashy motion, and no overly complicated front page. Avoid AI-scaffolding tells (uppercase tracked eyebrows, numbered section markers, hero-metric templates, side-stripe borders) even where the Kontix reference uses them.

## Design Principles

- Prove, don't claim: every trust statement (quality, reliability, credentials) is backed by a real photo, stat, or testimonial rather than adjectives alone.
- Reduce friction to the phone call: the CTA and direct contact info stay visible and unavoidable throughout the scroll.
- Bold but uncluttered: strong typographic contrast and real photography carry the design; the page never crowds the visitor with more sections or copy than they need.
- Family-owned, not corporate: warmth and craftsmanship pride over polished-agency gloss.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Standard WCAG AA. Respect `prefers-reduced-motion` for any scroll/reveal animation. Ensure body text and CTA text meet AA contrast against any photography or tinted backgrounds used behind them.
